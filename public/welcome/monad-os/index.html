What it's for

Monad OS is built for the moment you're stepping into your next level: a launch, a leap, a choice between two paths, a goal bigger than anything you've done before. The person you've been got you here, but getting there asks you to become someone new. Same you, same results.

Most people arrive with plenty of vision and plenty of drive. What's missing is capacity: the ability to hold more without it spilling out as overthinking, over-giving, pushing or running in five directions at once. What you build can only grow as far as you can hold. The work expands what you can hold.

Why it's called Monad OS

The monad is one of the oldest symbols there is. Pythagoras called it the source of all numbers: the one, indivisible point that everything else comes from. In this work it stands for your essential self, the part of you that remains when the noise is removed. OS is your operating system, the beliefs and habits running in the background that decide how you think, feel and act. Monad OS updates that system so it runs from that self.

What's included

Monad Activations10 on the calendar each month

Live group energy sessions, 1.5 hours each, and the heart of the work. To become someone new, your identity has to change, and that's what the activations are designed to do: shift your state and your energy so the change can happen. You join at least four, and ideally as many as you can.

WorkbooksOne before each session

Four workbooks, each one a layer deeper than the last. You answer them out loud, as a voice note, which gets you out of your head and past the internal editor. They help you process what comes up in the activations. It's my take on strategic shadow work, because integration is where the change lands.

Strategic sessionsFour private, 45 minutes

This is where things move. I prepare for each one with your workbook recording, and because the activations and workbooks are already doing their part, we move at a very different pace. We turn what you've seen into decisions and clear next steps.

WhatsAppThroughout

Support between sessions, directly with me.

How it works

Most of your day runs on autopilot. The same thoughts bring the same feelings, the same feelings drive the same choices, and the same choices give you the same results. That's why you can understand a pattern perfectly and still repeat it.

In an activation your brain slows down and your heart and brain fall into sync, and in that state the autopilot can be rewritten. You get to feel what it's like to be the next version of you before it has happened, and the more often you feel it, the more natural it becomes.

The month goes round in a circle rather than a straight line. You come back to the same things, and each time you go a little deeper.

  • Activations shift your state and your identity.
  • Workbooks help you process what comes up, and often you'll hear your own answers as you speak.
  • Strategic sessions turn what you've seen into decisions and clear next steps.

The answers come from you. My role is to hold up the mirror, so you can see what you already know.

The shape of the month

Week 1 · Where you are

Taking stock, honestly. What you want, and what's been in the way.

Week 2 · What's been running you

The beliefs and patterns underneath, where they came from, and what they've been protecting.

Week 3 · Who you're becoming

The version of you that already holds what you want, and how it feels to be them.

Week 4 · How you build from here

Decisions, structure and how you show up, from a steadier place.

What it asks of you

The work only works if you do it. That means all four strategic sessions within the 30 days, at least four activations, and each workbook recorded before its session.
